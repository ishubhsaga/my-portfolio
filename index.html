About

</> frontend ✦ AI UI ◐ motion

I'm a front-end developer based in Delhi, currently building product UI at PiHex Labs and completing my MCA at Chandigarh University. My recent work centres on an AI-assisted medical imaging tool — building the slide viewer and result overlays clinicians actually interact with — alongside contributions to NiiVue, an open-source WebGL medical imaging viewer.

Outside of product work, I care about interfaces that stay out of the way: fast, accessible, and easy to trust. I'm comfortable across the modern front-end stack — React, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind — and I bring a background in video editing and motion design to how I think about animation and visual polish.
